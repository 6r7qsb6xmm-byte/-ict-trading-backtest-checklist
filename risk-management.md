# Risk Management

## Position-sizing principle

The amount risked should be determined before entering a trade.

Basic formula:

Position size = Amount at risk / Loss per unit at the stop loss

## Example

Account balance: $5,000

Risk: 1%

Amount at risk:

$5,000 × 0.01 = $50

The correct lot size then depends on:

- Instrument
- Contract size
- Tick value
- Stop-loss distance
- Broker specifications

## Rules

- Check the symbol specifications before calculating lot size.
- Never assume that two brokers use the same contract size.
- Recalculate when the stop-loss distance changes.
- Respect the broker’s minimum volume and volume step.
