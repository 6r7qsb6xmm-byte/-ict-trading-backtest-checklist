# Backtesting Checklist

## 1. Trading session

- Trade only during the selected New York session.
- Record the date and exact entry time.
- Avoid entering outside the defined session.

## 2. Higher-timeframe context

- Check the 1-hour chart.
- Identify previous-day high and low.
- Identify session highs and lows.
- Mark clear liquidity above or below price.
- Do not force a directional bias when conditions are unclear.

## 3. Liquidity sweep

For a long setup:

- Price must sweep sell-side liquidity.
- The sweep must occur during or shortly before the trading window.
- Price must reject the swept level.

For a short setup:

- Price must sweep buy-side liquidity.
- The sweep must occur during or shortly before the trading window.
- Price must reject the swept level.

## 4. Market structure shift

- Move to the 1-minute chart.
- Wait for displacement away from the sweep.
- Require a candle close beyond a relevant short-term swing.
- Do not count a wick alone as confirmation.

## 5. Entry

- Enter after the confirmed market structure shift.
- Use a fair value gap or retracement when available.
- Do not chase price after a large move.

## 6. Stop loss

For a long:

- Place the stop below the swept low or confirmed swing low.

For a short:

- Place the stop above the swept high or confirmed swing high.

- Add a small buffer when needed.
- Skip the trade if the stop is unreasonably large.

## 7. Take profit

Possible targets:

- Opposing liquidity
- Previous-day high or low
- London session high or low
- Intraday equal highs or lows

- Define the target before entering.
- Record the planned risk-to-reward ratio.

## 8. Risk management

- Risk a fixed percentage per trade.
- Calculate position size before entry.
- Do not increase risk after a loss.
- Do not move the stop farther away.

## 9. Review

Record:

- Screenshot before entry
- Entry price
- Stop-loss price
- Target price
- Risk-to-reward ratio
- Result
- Whether every rule was respected
- Notes about execution mistakes
